Applied Case

During a data analysis and audit engagement, I developed an AI-assisted research process using Gemini Deep Research to investigate and classify agrochemical products based on public regulatory sources. The method organized findings, surfaced the supporting sources, and helped identify the manufacturer for each product.

Results were later reviewed manually by two professionals and showed strong agreement with the traditional process, reducing an activity that previously took several hours to approximately 15 minutes.
